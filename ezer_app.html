Step 2 of 3
How much should EZER act on its own?
You can change this anytime. Start cautious and graduate to more autonomy as you see EZER work.
Safe mode — I review everything first
Every action EZER wants to take goes to your approval queue. Nothing sends without your say-so. Best for getting started.
High-risk only — routine things go, big decisions come to me
Check-ins and health emails send automatically. Escalations and large retention offers need your approval.
Fully automatic — EZER handles everything
EZER acts on every customer without interruption. Review the results in the dashboard. Best once you trust the system.

Connections
Connect your tools and EZER starts watching your customers automatically. Each connector takes under 5 minutes.
Stripe
Pulls subscription status, MRR, payment failures, and billing history.
Setup: 2 min · dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys
Razorpay
Pulls payment data and subscription history. Best for India-based billing.
Setup: 2 min · dashboard.razorpay.com/app/keys
HubSpot
Pulls contacts, companies, deals, and engagement data. Adds full CRM context to every customer score.
Setup: 5 min · app.hubspot.com/private-apps
Intercom
Pulls support conversations and sentiment. Catches frustrated customers before they escalate.
Setup: 3 min · app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/keys
Salesforce
Pulls accounts, contacts, opportunities, and activity history. Best for teams already using Salesforce as their CRM.
Setup: 10 min · Requires Connected App credentials
